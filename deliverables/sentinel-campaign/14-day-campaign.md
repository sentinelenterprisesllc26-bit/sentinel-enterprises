## Paid-offer release checklist (HOLD)

Do not replace the free-resource CTA with an offer CTA until the owner independently verifies every item below:

1. Confirm the exact paid bundle contents, file names, version, format, and price in the source of truth.
2. Confirm that each promised file is actually accessible in the fulfillment path, with a successful test purchase or controlled test delivery.
3. Confirm who fulfills delivery, when access is granted, what happens on failure, and who handles support/refunds.
4. Confirm checkout, payment, order, thank-you, and receipt records match the actual product—not merely a click or a static thank-you page.
5. Confirm all offer language, legal/tax/financial boundaries, privacy language, and affiliate disclosures with the owner.
6. Confirm no CTA promises income, a result, instant access, email delivery, or contents that have not been verified.
7. Record the verification date, reviewer, evidence location, and rollback contact. Keep this record separate from campaign analytics.

## Measurement and attribution notes

Use the existing GA4 taxonomy where applicable: `resource_download_click` with resource and placement; `lead_form_success` only for an actual successful form; `product_checkout_click` for a checkout click; `affiliate_link_click` only when a disclosed affiliate link is actually used; and `social_link_click` for social navigation. Preserve UTM source (`youtube`, `tiktok`, `facebook`), medium (`organic_social`), campaign (`crypto_inheritance_14d`), and each day’s content value.

At weekly review, read UTM dimensions and GA4 events together. Treat clicks, page views, resource-download interactions, and qualified engagement as different measures. Reconcile sales, payment status, refunds, and commissions from real records separately. Orders are not clicks; a static thank-you page is not proof of a completed payment; and do not fabricate conversions. YouTube/TikTok profile-link attribution is inherently limited for old videos if the bio link rotates. Record the active bio UTM and the rotation date, and interpret older posts accordingly.
